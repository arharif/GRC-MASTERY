## Executive Summary & Cheat Sheet

### Final memory principle — Module 003

> **Own. Challenge. Assure.**  
> Three responsibilities that must work together without undermining accountability or independence.

| **First Line** | **Second Line** | **Third Line** |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **OWN** | **CHALLENGE** | **ASSURE** |

### 4.1 Five key takeaways

1. **First Line = Own and manage risk.** Business and operational management run processes and controls.
2. **Second Line = Support and challenge.** Risk and compliance functions help management identify weaknesses and monitor exposure.
3. **Third Line = Independent assurance.** Internal Audit evaluates governance, risks and controls without assuming ordinary management responsibility.
4. **The board oversees all three contributions.** It needs reliable information, transparent escalation and clear accountability.
5. **The lines must cooperate.** Independence does not mean isolation, and cooperation must not compromise objectivity.

### 4.2 Essential terminology

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Risk owner** | Person with responsibility and authority for managing a particular risk |
| **Control owner** | Person accountable for implementing or maintaining a specific control |
| **Oversight** | Monitoring, review and challenge |
| **Independence** | Freedom from interference or inappropriate responsibilities that compromise objective judgement |
| **Assurance** | Evidence-based evaluation intended to increase confidence |
| **RACI** | Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed |
| **Escalation** | Raising a matter to the appropriate decision-making authority |
| **Conflict of interest** | A circumstance that could undermine impartial judgement |
